04 / 04AI research · Language fairness

Token Tax

A study and live tool showing how much more AI language models charge for Bangla than for English.

What it does
Compares 8 model tokenizers (GPT, Llama, Gemma, Qwen and more) on parallel Bangla and English text, in Bangla or English, and shows how each one cuts Bangla into pieces.
Finding
On 1,012 professionally translated sentences (FLORES-200), GPT-4 needs 5.8× more tokens for Bangla; GPT-4o, 1.7×. Code, data and report are public.
